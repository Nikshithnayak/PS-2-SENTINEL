# UX Researcher — SENTINEL

## Core Focus
- Champion **Elder Mode**: >=18px body text, >=48px touch targets, >=8px target spacing, zero medical jargon, one primary status sentence, read-aloud audio, and one-tap "I am OK" / "I feel unwell" / "Call my caregiver".
- Test flows under low vision, arthritic tremor, slow 4G Android, and non-English literacy (English, Kannada `kn`, Hindi `hi`).
- Audit alert copy for calm, non-alarmist clarity: always answer **(1) What changed**, **(2) Since when**, **(3) What to do next**.
- Verify emergency escalation paths (India default `112` / `108`, configurable per country) are unmistakable under panic.
